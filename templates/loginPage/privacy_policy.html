Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Liver Cirrhosis Project ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you visit or use our website (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you:

  • Personal Data: While using our Service (such as registering an account), we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you, including your name, email address, date of birth, and gender.
  • Clinical Data: If you use our Liver Cirrhosis Predictor, we collect the laboratory metrics and parameters you input (e.g., Bilirubin, Albumin, Copper, Platelets) to calculate the cirrhosis stage and generate records/reports.
  • Log and Usage Data: We collect information that your browser sends whenever you visit our Service. This may include your IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent on those pages, and other diagnostic data.

2. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track the activity on our Service and store certain information. Cookies are files with a small amount of data which may include an anonymous unique identifier.

Google AdSense and Cookies

We partner with Google AdSense to serve advertisements on our Service. To serve these ads, Google uses technologies like cookies and web beacons. Please note the following regarding Google's data practices:

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site.
  • Google's use of the DoubleClick cookie enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of the use of the DoubleClick cookie for interest-based advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings page.

You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, if you do not accept cookies, you may not be able to use some portions of our Service.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data for various purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and secure our Service, including user account management.
  • To compute staging assessments using our machine learning models and generate analytical dashboards.
  • To allow you to participate in interactive features, such as chatting with our AI health consultant, Dr. Qwen.
  • To display relevant advertisements through Google AdSense.

4. Data Protection and Security

The security of your data is of critical importance to us. We employ state-of-the-art industry-standard safeguards, including AES-256 bit encryption, to protect your personal and clinical records. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Third-Party Services and Partners

Our Service contains links to third-party services and uses partners like Google for ad serving. For more detailed information on how Google collects, uses, and shares your data, we encourage you to read the policy document: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us by email at support@livercirrhosis.org.
